Ratan Tata

1937 – 2024

Summary

Born: 28 December 1937

Died: 9 October 2024

Education: B.Arch., Cornell University; Advanced Management Program, Harvard Business School

Awards: Padma Bhushan (2000), Padma Vibhushan (2008)

Life Timeline

  • Born in Bombay (now Mumbai), India, on December 28, 1937.
  • Studied architecture at Cornell University and completed the Advanced Management Program at Harvard Business School in 1975.
  • Joined the Tata Group in 1962, starting at Tata Steel.
  • Became Chairman of Tata Sons in 1991, leading the group until 2012 and briefly returning in 2016–2017.

Social Work

  1. Donated generously to public health and education initiatives, including significant contributions during national disasters.
  2. Under his leadership, Tata Trusts supported schools, hospitals, and rural development across India.
  3. Supported scientific research and social innovation in the country.

Leadership and Contributions

Ratan Tata's visionary leadership transformed the Tata Group into a global enterprise. Key milestones include:

  • Acquisition of Jaguar Land Rover, expanding Tata Motors' global reach.
  • Launch of the Tata Nano, the world’s cheapest car, making personal transport accessible.
  • Emphasis on corporate ethics and social responsibility across Tata businesses.

Philanthropy and Recognition

Ratan Tata was known for his humility, generosity, and impact beyond business:

  • Major philanthropic work through Tata Trusts in education, health, and livelihood sectors.
  • Recipient of India’s highest civilian honors – Padma Bhushan and Padma Vibhushan.
  • Featured in Forbes and other global publications for his leadership and philanthropy.
